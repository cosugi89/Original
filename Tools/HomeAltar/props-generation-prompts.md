# 小道具の隠れた面の補描プロンプト

組み込み `image_gen` を使用し、各既存小道具PNGを参照、透明背景を指定した。生成結果は `lectern-restored-underlay.png` と `memorial-restored-underlay.png` に保存した。実際の見える面は元画像を保持し、葉に隠れる補完面だけに生成結果を使用する。

## 書見台

Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset type: transparent Unity game sprite hidden-surface restoration. Edit target: supplied original wooden lectern with a brown book and gold gear decoration. Remove ONLY all green plant leaves and green stems at the bottom left and bottom right of the lectern. Restore the wood base corners/outline underneath those leaves if concealed, matching the original perspective. Keep the book, gear, gold metal corners, page edges, wood pillar, pedestal and ALL existing visible surfaces EXACTLY in their original pixel position, silhouette, size, perspective and colors. The input is 1199x1312 pixels, keep identical transparent canvas and registration. Original style: bold dark brown rounded outline and flat cream/gold/brown cartoon cel shading. Do not change the book or add details, seams or lighting. No foliage anywhere, no floor, cast shadow, background, text, grid, labels, glow or watermark. This restoration will only supply hidden material where the removed green plants concealed the base; all original unoccluded pixels will be retained.

## 戦果碑

Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset type: transparent Unity game sprite hidden-surface restoration. Edit target: supplied original stone achievement memorial with its gold wreath and star plaque. Remove ONLY the GREEN plant leaves and stems protruding from the left and right lower sides and foot of the memorial. KEEP the GOLD laurel/wreath leaves engraved on the plaque, star, plaque, rivets, moss stains on the stones and every visible stone block EXACTLY unchanged. Restore any concealed stone pedestal corner/outline underneath the removed green leaves with the existing perspective and cream top / gray front faces. Input is 1312x1199 pixels, keep identical transparent canvas and accurate pixel registration. Preserve original thick dark rounded outline, shape, colors, scale, chips, 2-3 tone cartoon shading. Do not add stones, extra joints, change the inscription or make a new illustration. No green vegetation, floor, shadow outside existing silhouette, background, text, grid, labels, glow or watermark. This restoration is only for hidden stone material; every unoccluded original pixel will remain untouched.
