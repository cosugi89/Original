# 祭壇補完素材の生成プロンプト

組み込み `image_gen` を使用し、透明背景を指定した。以下は実際に指定したプロンプト。参照は1枚目が既存祭壇PNG、2枚目が生成前の36石のコンタクトシート。

## 葉の下に隠れた面の補完 (`restored-underlay.png`)

Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset type: hidden-surface restoration underlay for a Unity altar assembly sprite set. Edit target: the supplied transparent original stone altar. Remove only ALL green leaves, stems and vegetation, and restore the stone areas that those leaves conceal. Keep every existing stone, stair, central oval platform, broken left column and broken right arch in EXACTLY the same pixel position, proportions, perspective, size and arrangement. The right broken arch must retain its three tilted upper blocks; do not make a complete arch. Preserve original cream top faces, gray front faces, thick dark rounded outline and simple flat 2–3-tone cartoon shading. Restore missing contours naturally beneath removed leaves with matching stone surfaces and dark edges. Do not add new stones or extra seams on visible surfaces. Keep the transparent canvas extent identical to the input. No ground, background, labels, text, grid, lighting glow or shadow outside the existing silhouette. This is a reconstruction layer used ONLY underneath the exact original visible pixels, so accurate registration and natural material continuity are essential.

## 独立石材の閉じた輪郭 (`closed-stones-atlas.png`)

Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset type: production Unity sprite atlas containing 36 individual stones, six columns by six rows, on a transparent 1152x1056 canvas. Edit target is the provided contact sheet. Keep EXACTLY the same 36 objects, cell locations, scale, camera perspective, surface colors and recognizable visible surfaces. Repair every object into a COMPLETE CLOSED INDIVIDUAL STONE SILHOUETTE: remove stray specks; close missing outline segments; fill bites left by adjacent objects and plants; restore naturally rounded cream top faces and gray front/side faces where a top stone previously occluded them. In particular the V-shaped column shafts (row 3 col 5 and row 5 col 4) need a natural cream top plane closing the V; V-shaped plinths (row3 col4,row5 col1,row5 col3) must be actual single broad low stone bases; the central oval floor (row2 col1) is a broad low slab with a very shallow gray front thickness; front rim stones with stair notches (row2 col3,col5) need closed lower front edges. Add ONLY a small amount of material needed to close hidden portions. Do not enlarge the objects overall or change their tilt. Preserve all top details, chips, shading and original pixel positions as closely as possible. Each object must read as one free-standing mobile fantasy game stone, NOT an image fragment. Flat cream and gray cel shading with 2-3 values, bold rounded dark brown to black contour, matching reference style. Leave all 6x6 transparent gutters completely empty. Do not draw a background, grid, labels, numbers, text, frames, foliage, glow or cast shadows. This is a restoration layer used under the exact original visible pixels of each stone.
